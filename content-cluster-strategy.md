## What Is a Content Cluster Strategy?

A content cluster is a group of interlinked blog posts all focused on a central topic.

- The **Pillar Blog** is the main, comprehensive article targeting the primary keyword
- **Supporting Blogs** each target a related long-tail keyword and link back to the pillar
- **Internal Links** flow in both directions, building topical authority in Google's eyes

This strategy is how sites like HubSpot, Backlinko, and Ahrefs dominate their niches.

## Publication Order & Reasoning

Publish supporting blogs BEFORE the pillar. This is the professional SEO strategy.

Why: When the pillar blog is published, it immediately has existing pages to link to, increasing its internal link equity. Google also sees that you have already covered the topic deeply across multiple pages — a signal of topical authority.

| Week | Publish | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Blog 3: Cost Guide | High transactional intent; easy to rank; attracts bottom-funnel visitors |
| Week 2 | Blog 1: SEO Services | High commercial intent; establishes core service authority |
| Week 3 | Blog 2: Social Media | Broad audience; builds brand awareness traffic |
| Week 4 | Blog 4: How to Choose | Decision-stage; builds trust with comparison shoppers |
| Week 5 | Blog 5: Google Ads vs SEO | Informational; attracts research-stage audience |
| Week 6 | PILLAR BLOG | Published last with all supporting blogs ready to interlink |

## Local SEO Integration

Every blog in this cluster includes:
- City name (Hyderabad) in the H1 heading
- City + neighborhood mentions in the body (Gachibowli, Banjara Hills, HITEC City, etc.)
- Meta titles and descriptions with location keyword
- A section or mention of areas served
- Google Business Profile optimization recommendation

This ensures every page in the cluster sends local relevance signals to Google.

## How to Measure Success

Track these KPIs monthly:

- Organic impressions and clicks (Google Search Console)
- Keyword rankings for each target keyword (weekly check)
- Blog traffic (Google Analytics)
- Leads from blog pages (contact form submissions, call button clicks)
- Average position improvement over time

Expected benchmarks:
- Month 1–2: Indexing, first impressions
- Month 3–4: Rankings appearing for supporting blog keywords
- Month 5–6: Pillar blog begins ranking; cluster traffic growing
- Month 6+: Compounding growth in organic leads
